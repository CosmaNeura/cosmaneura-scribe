# Compact recorder and responsive preparation

Tier 2. The owner approved clinician UX implementation, local validation, merge,
staging delivery and idle same-identity native updates. Production is excluded.

## Delivery

Deploy the compatible Core first/continuation receivers and Scribe continuation
endpoint before new client bundles. The clients retain complete encrypted request
recipes before dispatch and preserve older pending-journal recovery. Existing
active-recording renewal remains unchanged. Install Mac and iPhone bundles only
while idle; preserve signing IDs, encrypted queues, keys and journals.

The compact Mac recorder is the primary control surface over a meeting. The
mandatory operating-system recording indicator remains. Show-window recovery
reveals the same pending chooser and cannot select a window or start audio.
Input meters consume accepted PCM metadata, are rate limited and expire; they do
not substitute animation or source-start status for detected audio.

On interruption, finish physical source closure, encrypted persistence and the
original stop confirmation before enabling Resume. Concurrent Finish may upgrade
the pending stop to upload, but automatic interruption handling never uploads.
Uncertain replies retain exact original recovery. Do not bypass retirement,
consent, current authority or custody to meet a timing target.

## Verification and recovery

Validate lifecycle, receipt replay, interruption/Finish races, native pipe/source
ownership, real signal metadata and visible chooser/stop states. Keep exact source
hashes with timings. Simulated normal start/Resume below four seconds does not
prove physical performance. Full interruption plus immediate Resume can exceed
four seconds; expose the actual confirmation phase while it completes.

Run controlled synthetic device acceptance only with quiet conditions and no
Device Hub/mirroring. Preserve and exclude the unclassified September29 run.
Rollback keeps compatible receivers and all retained encrypted state. Never
reset/uninstall or discard uncertain requests as a recovery shortcut.
